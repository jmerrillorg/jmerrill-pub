# Agentic Boundary

This packet exposes no payment mutation to an agent. It does not add a capability-registry entry, A3 effect, A4 orchestration authority, or raw Stripe tool access.

Future supervised agents may read status, explain a blocked correlation, prepare evidence, and surface an exception. They may not create payments, invoices, refunds, transfers, customer records, or lifecycle effects. Agent/capability registry authority remains `jmerrillorg/jm1-ops`.
