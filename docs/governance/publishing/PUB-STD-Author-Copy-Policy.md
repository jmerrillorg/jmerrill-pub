# PUB-STD: Author Copy Policy

Version: 1.0

Status: CANONICAL GOVERNANCE STANDARD

Effective date: 2026-08-06

Authority: OR-2026-003 governance remediation

## Purpose

This standard establishes the governed complimentary author-copy policy for J Merrill Publishing package benefits.

Approved package benefits must be discoverable from repository documentation. This document is the single governed source of truth for complimentary author-copy quantities and delivery timing.

## Scope

This policy applies to complimentary author copies included with J Merrill Publishing package-based publishing offers, including Starter, Professional, Premier Publishing Package, and JM Signature traditional publishing track references.

This policy does not replace separately purchased author-copy orders, bulk author-copy order support, shipping charges, royalty terms, publication rights, or separately approved package amendments.

## Canonical Complimentary Copy Policy

| Package | Paperback | Hardcover | eBook |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Starter | 5 | 0 | 1 |
| Professional | 10 | 2 | 1 |
| Premier Publishing Package | 15 | 5 | 1 |
| JM Signature traditional publishing track | 15 | 5 | 1 |

## Delivery Timing

Paperback copies are delivered after the paperback edition is published and available for author-copy fulfillment.

Hardcover copies are delivered once the hardcover edition is published and available for author-copy fulfillment.

eBook copies are delivered upon digital publication.

## Hardcover Conditions

Hardcover complimentary copies apply only when the package includes a hardcover benefit or when the hardcover edition has otherwise been approved and published.

Starter includes no complimentary hardcover copies.

Professional includes two complimentary hardcover copies.

Premier Publishing Package includes five complimentary hardcover copies.

JM Signature traditional publishing track includes five complimentary hardcover copies when the traditional publishing agreement grants the author-copy benefit.

## eBook Delivery

Each package includes one complimentary eBook copy delivered when the digital edition is published.

The eBook copy is a delivery benefit, not a separate Product Form approval. The digital edition must still follow the approved title and edition process.

## Replacement Policy

Replacement copies are not automatically included in the complimentary-copy benefit.

Damaged, lost, misdelivered, or replacement copies must be reviewed case by case and may require a separately approved author-copy order or support action.
