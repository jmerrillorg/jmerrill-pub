# PUB-STD: Author Copy Policy

Version: 1.1

Status: CANONICAL GOVERNANCE STANDARD

Effective date: 2026-08-06

Authority: OR-2026-003 governance remediation; Amendment 1 - Elected Product Form Entitlement Rule

## Purpose

This standard establishes the governed complimentary author-copy and author-delivery entitlement policy for J Merrill Publishing package benefits.

Approved package benefits must be discoverable from repository documentation. This document is the single governed source of truth for complimentary author-copy quantities, delivery timing, and the Product Form election rule.

## Scope

This policy applies to complimentary author copies and author-delivery entitlements included with J Merrill Publishing package-based publishing offers, including Starter, Professional, Premier Publishing Package, and JM Signature traditional publishing track references.

This policy does not replace separately purchased author-copy orders, bulk author-copy order support, shipping charges, royalty terms, publication rights, Product Form eligibility, edition-slot counts, package prices, or separately approved package amendments.

Executed agreements and addenda are preserved as executed. This amended policy applies to future agreements, unexecuted package addenda, and title production planning when consistent with the signed terms. Any conflict with already executed terms must be flagged for Jackie review.

## Controlling Rule

Complimentary author entitlements follow the title's approved and elected Product Forms.

The package or track determines the quantity.

The elected Product Forms determine which editions receive the quantity.

Only elected Product Forms receive entitlements. Unelected, retired, cancelled, inactive, or unapproved Product Forms receive no complimentary copies or delivery entitlement.

Later-added Product Forms receive the applicable entitlement only after the add-on or election is approved and the Product Form is published or delivered.

## Package Allocation Authority

| Package / Track | Print allocation per elected print Product Form | Digital entitlement per elected digital Product Form | Audio entitlement |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Starter | 5 | 1 | 1 author delivery |
| Professional | 10 | 1 | 1 author delivery |
| Premier Publishing Package | 15 | 1 | 1 author delivery |
| JM Signature traditional publishing track | 15 | 1 | 1 author delivery |

Premier Publishing Package and JM Signature traditional publishing track are separate commercial authorities even though their current entitlement quantities match.

## Product Form Delivery Class

Use the existing Product Form authority. Do not create sub-forms, narration sub-forms, PF-05C, or competing Product Form definitions for author-copy purposes.

| Product Form | Delivery Class | Entitlement Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PF-01 Paperback | PRINT | Receives the package print allocation when elected and published. |
| PF-02 Hardcover | PRINT | Receives the package print allocation when elected and published. |
| PF-03 Standard Digital EPUB | DIGITAL | Receives one complimentary digital entitlement when elected and published. |
| PF-04 Audiobook | AUDIO | Receives one complimentary author delivery or redemption entitlement after publication. |
| PF-05 Large Print | PRINT | Receives the package print allocation when elected and published. |
| PF-06 Complex-Content Accessibility Edition | DIGITAL | Receives one complimentary digital entitlement when delivered digitally. A separately approved physical edition must follow its approved delivery class. |
| PF-07 Vertical Graphic Edition | INACTIVE / NO ENTITLEMENT | Must not generate complimentary-copy entitlements. |
| PF-08 Interactive / Multimedia | DIGITAL | Receives one complimentary digital entitlement only when elected, contracted, completed, delivered, and scope-approved. |

Future approved Product Forms must be classified as PRINT, DIGITAL, AUDIO, or INACTIVE before complimentary entitlements are generated.

## Print Product Forms

For each elected print Product Form:

- Starter: 5 copies
- Professional: 10 copies
- Premier Publishing Package: 15 copies
- JM Signature traditional publishing track: 15 copies

Current print Product Forms include PF-01 Paperback, PF-02 Hardcover, PF-05 Large Print, and any future approved Product Form classified as print.

## Digital Product Forms

For each elected digital Product Form:

- 1 complimentary digital entitlement

Current digital Product Forms include PF-03 Standard Digital EPUB, PF-06 Complex-Content Accessibility Edition when delivered digitally, PF-08 Interactive / Multimedia when delivered digitally and scope-approved, and any future approved Product Form classified as digital.

## Audio Product Forms

For PF-04 Audiobook:

- 1 complimentary author delivery or redemption entitlement after publication

This is not a physical copy.

## Examples

| Package / Track | Elected Product Forms | Complimentary Entitlements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | PF-01 Paperback + PF-03 Standard Ebook | 5 paperback copies + 1 standard ebook entitlement |
| Starter | PF-01 Paperback + PF-05 Large Print | 5 paperback copies + 5 large-print copies |
| Professional | PF-01 Paperback + PF-02 Hardcover + PF-04 Audiobook | 10 paperback copies + 10 hardcover copies + 1 audiobook author delivery |
| Premier Publishing Package | PF-05 Large Print + PF-03 Standard Ebook + PF-08 Interactive / Multimedia | 15 large-print copies + 1 standard ebook entitlement + 1 PF-08 digital entitlement when scope-approved |

No unelected format receives complimentary copies.

## Delivery Timing

Print copies are delivered after the elected print Product Form is published and available for author-copy fulfillment.

Digital entitlements are delivered upon digital publication or approved digital delivery.

Audiobook author delivery or redemption entitlement is delivered after audiobook publication.

## Replacement Policy

Replacement copies are not automatically included in the complimentary-copy benefit.

Damaged, lost, misdelivered, or replacement copies must be reviewed case by case and may require a separately approved author-copy order or support action.
