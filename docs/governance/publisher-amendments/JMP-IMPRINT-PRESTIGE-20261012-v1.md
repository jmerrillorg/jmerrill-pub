## Operative ruling

JM Prestige is an additional commercial imprint alongside J Merrill Publishing, JM Little, JM Signature, JM Verse and JM Works. This amendment supersedes the five-commercial-imprint enumeration in JMP-GOV-v1.0 / CANON-CERT-001 only to the extent necessary to recognize JM Prestige. Historical records remain evidence of their respective versions, not the current complete enumeration.

JM Prestige Standard and JM Prestige Premium are its two publishing partnership tiers:

| Tier | Monthly payment | Twelve monthly payments | Paid annually |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Standard | $750 | $9,000 | $8,000 |
| Premium | $1,250 | $15,000 | $13,500 |

The twelve-payment totals are not the discounted annual-payment prices. These are preserved prices, not new pricing authority. References presenting JM Signature as the paid Premium tier are superseded by JM Prestige Premium. JM Signature remains a separate Publisher-designated imprint and is not purchasable; its existing eligibility rules remain intact.

## Review and execution boundaries

Existing application and Publisher-review criteria remain applicable. Manuscript quality, genre fit, a selector choice, or an advisory recommendation alone does not establish Prestige eligibility or an author's acceptance of a paid partnership. Title-specific partnership and assignment authority must be established separately before an assignment can change. Funding model, publishing package, editorial scope, imprint and contractual terms remain distinct.

This amendment changes no existing agreement, royalty, right, title assignment, funding arrangement, stage, campaign or author communication. It authorizes no new spend. The five fresh Jackie submissions retain their publisher-funded authorizations and manuscript safeguards; they are not paid Prestige enrollments by inference.

JM Legacy Editions remains the separately governed Class B preservation imprint. Recognition of Prestige as the sixth commercial imprint does not erase that noncommercial category or turn it into a retail offering.
