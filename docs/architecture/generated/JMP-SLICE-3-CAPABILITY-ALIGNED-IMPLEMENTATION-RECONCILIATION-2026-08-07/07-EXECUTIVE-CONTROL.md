# Executive Control / One-Operator Experience

Target: one daily surface answering what Jackie needs today, what is overdue, which authors are waiting, which titles are blocked, what money needs attention, what marketing opportunity fired, what distribution exception exists, what approval is required, and what systems can handle without Jackie.

Existing custom Publisher Operating Center classification: CONFIGURE_AROUND. Keep its business questions, but test Microsoft dashboards, Power Apps, Power BI, Teams, Outlook, and Approvals before expanding custom dashboard code.

| Microsoft option | Disposition | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dynamics dashboards | CONFIGURE | Strong fit for Commercial Operations, lead/opportunity/quote, and customer/account status. |
| Power BI | CONFIGURE | Strong fit for daily current-work, money, marketing, and exception readouts if source data is governed. |
| Power Apps | CONFIGURE | Strong fit for Jackie daily command surface and coordinator work queues. |
| Teams | CONFIGURE | Good notification surface, especially approvals and daily digest; should not become another inbox to monitor. |
| Outlook | USE_AS_IS | Appropriate for calendar/email, but not sufficient as system of record. |
| Copilot Studio | UNKNOWN | Do not depend on it until entitlement, governance, and risk posture are verified. |
| Adaptive Cards / Approvals | CONFIGURE | Good fit for routine approvals when evidence and authority are clear. |
