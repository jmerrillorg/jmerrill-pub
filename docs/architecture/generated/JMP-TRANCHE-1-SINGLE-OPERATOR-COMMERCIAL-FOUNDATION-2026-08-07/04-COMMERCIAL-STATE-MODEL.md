# Commercial State Model

Plain-language business states before publishing starts. No schema is created by this model.

| State | Business meaning | Owner | Entry condition | Exit condition | Jackie approval required? | Customer communication required? | Evidence required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| INQUIRY_RECEIVED | Potential author/title inquiry exists but is not qualified. | Commercial operator / system route | Website form or manual intake captured. | Accepted for qualification or declined/withdrawn. | NO, unless sensitive or exceptional. | Optional acknowledgement only if approved. | Inquiry record/source timestamp. |
| QUALIFICATION_REQUIRED | Inquiry needs business review before sales pursuit. | Jackie or delegated commercial reviewer | Inquiry has minimum contact/title information. | Qualified, declined, or more information requested. | YES. | Only if requesting more information. | Qualification note and owner. |
| QUALIFIED | JMP is willing to explore offer/track/package. | Jackie / Commercial Operations | Jackie approves commercial pursuit. | Opportunity created or offer prep begins. | YES. | Not required until outreach/offer. | Qualification approval. |
| OFFER_PREPARED | Package/track/PF/pricing offer is drafted internally. | Commercial Operations | Catalog-backed package/track/PF/pricing selected. | Offer sent or revised/held. | YES for exceptions; otherwise REVIEW. | NO until sent. | Catalog/pricing references and draft quote. |
| OFFER_SENT | Offer or quote has been sent for prospect decision. | Commercial Operations | Approved offer delivered. | Accepted, revised, declined, or expired. | NO unless special terms. | YES. | Sent offer/quote evidence. |
| AGREEMENT_PENDING | Correct agreement version is generated/sent but not executed. | Commercial Operations | Offer accepted and agreement generated. | Executed, revised, withdrawn, or expired. | REVIEW before issuance. | YES if sent. | Template/version/artifact manifest. |
| AGREEMENT_EXECUTED | Executed agreement exists and version is preserved. | Commercial Operations | Signed/accepted agreement stored. | Payment pending/confirmed or hold. | NO unless terms exception. | YES if countersignature/receipt is part of process. | Executed agreement artifact/version checksum. |
| PAYMENT_PENDING | Required payment is not yet confirmed. | Commercial Operations / system | Agreement or payment request exists. | Payment confirmed, failed, partial, stale, or waived by approved exception. | NO for routine status; YES for exception. | Payment reminder only if approved. | Stripe/payment request reference. |
| PAYMENT_CONFIRMED | Required payment condition is satisfied. | System projection / Jackie review exceptions | Stripe confirms successful required payment or approved alternate proof exists. | Fulfillment authorization evaluation. | NO unless manual correction. | NO. | Stripe event/payment ID/status evidence. |
| FULFILLMENT_AUTHORIZED | Publishing operations may begin under approved track/package/PF scope. | Dataverse projection / Jackie exception owner | Agreement valid + payment satisfied + package/track valid + required intake fields complete. | Publishing operations begins or hold. | NO for standard; YES for override. | Internal only in Tranche 1. | Gate evaluation evidence. |
| ON_HOLD | Commercial lifecycle paused for explicit reason. | Jackie / Commercial Operations | Missing evidence, exception, expired offer, failed payment, special terms, or executive hold. | Resolved, declined, withdrawn, or reactivated. | YES. | Only if needed. | Hold reason, owner, expiration/restoration criteria. |
| DECLINED | JMP declines or prospect is not commercially accepted. | Jackie | Decision to decline. | Closed. | YES. | Optional. | Decision note. |
| WITHDRAWN | Prospect withdraws or no longer proceeds. | Commercial Operations | Author/prospect withdrawal or inactivity close rule. | Closed or reopened by new inquiry. | NO unless sensitive. | Optional. | Withdrawal or inactivity evidence. |
