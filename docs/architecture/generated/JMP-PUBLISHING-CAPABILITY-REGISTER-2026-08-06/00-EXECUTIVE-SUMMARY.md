## High-Risk Findings

- The current owner field confirms the delegation problem: Jackie is the exact current operational owner for 15 of 38 inventoried capabilities, and Jackie is also named as a shared approver or reviewer across many additional rows.
- Most prior command centers should be absorbed into the nine-capability model rather than retained as standalone products.
- Strategic Marketing exists as design/proof fragments, but successor-facing operating coverage is not complete.
- Post-Publication Operations must remain distinct from Financial Operations because royalties, complimentary copies, retirement, reversion, and ongoing title management are lifecycle-triggered even when money moves.
- The Products and Services Catalog v2.1 parse failure remains explicitly flagged and was not silently corrected.
