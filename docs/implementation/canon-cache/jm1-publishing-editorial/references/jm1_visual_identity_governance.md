## Visual Rules

- Use only the palette of the publishing entity.
- Do not borrow Jackie Smith, Jr. colors for JM1 or branch content.
- Do not use `#FFD700` on enterprise or branch visuals.
- Do not invent additional brand colors.
- Use dark surfaces only when the composition benefits from them.
- Favor editorial, symbolic, architectural, documentary, or
  concept-driven imagery over generic stock imagery.
- Avoid excessive text embedded in generated images.
- Avoid logos unless supplied or explicitly requested.
- Do not fabricate branded signage, uniforms, facilities, people, or
  products.
- Maintain visual restraint and professional coherence.
- Every image must support the article's meaning without adding a new
  claim.
- Generate literal accessibility alt text separately from the caption.
