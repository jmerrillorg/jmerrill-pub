# JMP Cover Genre Guide

**Status:** CANON CACHE | Manual canon synchronized 2026-08-20

Cover strategy must begin with the reader promise of the genre and imprint.
Visual direction should support audience recognition before decorative novelty.

## Review Factors

- genre and subgenre;
- comparable shelf / retail context;
- reader expectation;
- tone and emotional promise;
- imprint fit;
- title and subtitle hierarchy;
- author brand fit;
- market distinctiveness without category confusion.

## Boundary

Genre fit informs the cover concept. It does not approve the full wrap or final
production cover.
