# OR-2026-003 Governance Decision

Date: 2026-08-06

Decision: establish `docs/governance/publishing/PUB-STD-Author-Copy-Policy.md` as the canonical governance standard for complimentary author-copy quantities and timing.

Amendment 1 Decision: complimentary author-copy and author-delivery entitlements follow the title's approved and elected Product Forms. Package or track determines quantity. Product Form election determines which editions receive the quantity.

## Canonical Policy

| Package / Track | Print allocation per elected print Product Form | Digital entitlement per elected digital Product Form | Audio entitlement |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Starter | 5 | 1 | 1 author delivery |
| Professional | 10 | 1 | 1 author delivery |
| Premier Publishing Package | 15 | 1 | 1 author delivery |
| JM Signature traditional publishing track | 15 | 1 | 1 author delivery |

Only elected Product Forms receive entitlements. Unelected formats receive no complimentary copies. PF-07 generates no entitlement. PF-08 generates one digital entitlement only when elected and scope-approved.

## Authority Boundary

This remediation establishes discoverable policy authority and aligns existing package/agreement references.

It does not authorize:

- runtime activation;
- client-title automation thaw;
- author communications;
- executed agreement reinterpretation or regeneration;
- commercial catalog mutation;
- Dataverse schema changes;
- Business Central changes;
- public website deployment.

## Governance Rationale

Approved package benefits must be discoverable through repository search. A policy that exists only in conversation history is not sufficiently governed for operational use.
