# OR-2026-0XX Governance Decision

Date: 2026-08-06

Decision: establish `docs/governance/publishing/PUB-STD-Author-Copy-Policy.md` as the canonical governance standard for complimentary author-copy quantities and timing.

## Canonical Policy

| Package | Paperback | Hardcover | eBook |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Starter | 5 | 0 | 1 |
| Professional | 10 | 2 | 1 |
| Premier / Signature | 15 | 5 | 1 |

Hardcover copies are delivered once the hardcover edition is published.

eBook copies are delivered upon digital publication.

## Authority Boundary

This remediation establishes discoverable policy authority and aligns existing package/agreement references.

It does not authorize:

- runtime activation;
- client-title automation thaw;
- author communications;
- agreement language redesign;
- commercial catalog mutation;
- Dataverse schema changes;
- Business Central changes;
- public package changes beyond correcting displayed complimentary-copy benefits to match the governed standard.

## Governance Rationale

Approved package benefits must be discoverable through repository search. A policy that exists only in conversation history is not sufficiently governed for operational use.
