# 08 Emergency and Continuity

## Jackie Unavailable: Two-Week Operating Playbook

### What May Continue

Trusted operators may continue:

- routine author follow-up using approved language;
- evidence filing;
- checklist completion;
- package preparation that has already been approved;
- production work that is already approved and does not change scope;
- distribution follow-up that checks status without changing public promises;
- royalty preparation that does not change payment terms;
- author-copy fulfillment under the current policy.

### What Must Pause

Pause:

- title acceptance or decline;
- Publishing Track changes;
- agreement exceptions;
- special terms;
- new package promises;
- Format & Title Lock;
- Correction Authorized;
- release exceptions;
- public-offer changes;
- royalty exceptions;
- client automation thaw;
- any action where authority is unclear.

### What an Operator May Approve

An operator may approve routine scheduling, evidence filing, standard status updates, checklist completion, and preparation of materials already within approved scope.

### What Requires Executive Authority

Executive authority is required for rights, royalties, agreements, package changes, title acceptance, public promises, release exceptions, corrections, and automation thaw.

### Author Communications

Communicate truthfully and narrowly:

- say what is known;
- say what is waiting;
- give realistic follow-up timing;
- do not promise a decision Jackie has not made;
- do not send sensitive or binding messages without approval.

### Vendor and Distributor Issues

Handle urgent vendor or distributor issues only to protect the existing title state. Correct obvious clerical issues when already authorized. Escalate anything that changes scope, price, rights, release date, or public presentation.

### Payments and Royalties

Protect payments and royalties by preserving records, preparing review packets, and avoiding changes to terms. Do not create exceptions or make disputed payments without executive authority.

### Evidence Preservation

Preserve:

- author messages;
- files sent and received;
- proof files;
- approvals already granted;
- distribution proof;
- payment and royalty records;
- issue reports;
- decisions held for Jackie.

### Return Briefing

When Jackie returns, provide:

- active title summary;
- actions completed;
- authors contacted;
- decisions held;
- risks or exceptions;
- files received;
- release, payment, or royalty obligations;
- recommended next decisions.

## Publishing Emergency Guide

### Author-Facing File Defect

Stop further sending. Preserve the defective file, identify recipients, prepare corrected file if authority exists, and escalate before sending a replacement.

### Wrong Attachment Sent

Preserve the sent message, identify recipient and attachment, notify Jackie, and prepare a correction message. Do not send a new file until approved.

### Distribution Listing Problem

Capture proof, identify affected edition, compare against approved metadata, and escalate if the fix affects title, price, author name, availability, or release timing.

### Rights Concern

Pause affected work, preserve the concern, identify the claimant or source, and route to Jackie. Do not publish, remove, or revise rights language without authority.

### Payment or Royalty Issue

Preserve records, identify the author and title, identify the amount and period, and route to Financial and Royalty Support. Do not improvise payment terms.

### Missing Source Manuscript

Pause editing or production. Identify candidate files, preserve them, and request confirmation of the controlling manuscript.

### Corrupted Production File

Stop using the file. Preserve the corrupted version, locate the last good proof or source, and notify the Production Coordinator.

### Public Metadata Error

Capture proof, compare against approved metadata, identify affected channels, and escalate if the correction changes anything beyond a clerical fix.

### Security or Privacy Concern

Pause affected communication, preserve evidence, limit sharing, and escalate immediately.
