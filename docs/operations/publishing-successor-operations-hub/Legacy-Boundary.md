# Legacy Boundary

Legacy material remains preserved.

Successors operate from the Human Operating Layer.

Legacy folders are reference-only unless the Current Authority Index points to them.

Do not rename, relocate, delete, or clean legacy content under this instruction.

## High-Risk Legacy Items

| Legacy Item | Why It Is Risky | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Older agreement drafts and generated agreement copies | They may look suitable for use but may not be the approved operational template. | Use only the two current agreement templates in Agreement Templates. |
| Generated catalog reconciliation files outside the final catalog register | They preserve history and validation but are not the daily operating answer. | Use the current catalog and pricing registers listed in the index. |
| Prior author-review packages | They may reflect old manuscript stages. | Use the active title current-state page to identify the controlling package. |
| Session archives and older project packages | They may contain useful evidence but are not designed for daily operation. | Do not browse them as a normal workflow. |
