# Role Playbooks

These playbooks define future delegation. They do not assume all roles are currently staffed.

## 1. President & CEO

Role Purpose: Holds final business authority for Publishing.

Daily Responsibilities: Review urgent decisions, author-sensitive issues, approvals, exceptions, and business commitments.

Weekly Responsibilities: Review active titles, blocked work, release obligations, agreement posture, and operational risk.

Monthly Responsibilities: Review royalties, portfolio health, package accuracy, title retirement candidates, and policy drift.

Approvals Held: Title acceptance, track, special terms, agreement exceptions, Format & Title Lock, Correction Authorized, release exceptions, public-offer changes, automation thaw, title closeout.

Approvals Not Held: Routine filing, standard checklist completion, routine scheduling, and ordinary preparation already within approved scope may be handled by operators.

Top SOPs: Accept or Decline a Publishing Project; Select the Correct Publishing Track; Approve the Editorial Master; Handle a Post-Release Correction; Close or Retire a Title.

Key Reports: Daily blockers, weekly active portfolio, monthly royalty and obligation review, quarterly successor readiness.

Escalation Path: Executive judgment; counsel or external owner when rights, legal, institutional, or payment risk requires it.

Continuity Expectations: Leave decisions, boundaries, and current title priorities discoverable.

## 2. Publishing Coordinator

Role Purpose: Keeps the publishing portfolio moving under approved policy.

Daily Responsibilities: Check author responses, update next actions, prepare routine materials, file evidence, and identify blockers.

Weekly Responsibilities: Prepare active title review, agreement readiness review, author waiting list, and upcoming obligations.

Monthly Responsibilities: Confirm current work hygiene, open author-copy obligations, and titles needing closeout attention.

Approvals Held: Routine follow-up, evidence filing, checklist completion, and standard status updates.

Approvals Not Held: Acceptance, track, special terms, agreement exceptions, release exceptions, corrections, royalties, public-offer changes, or automation thaw.

Top SOPs: Review a New Publishing Inquiry; Issue a Publishing Agreement; Start Author Onboarding; Send an Author Review Package; Confirm a Title Is Live.

Key Reports: Run Today, Current Work, weekly active portfolio review.

Escalation Path: Jackie for business authority; Editorial Lead for manuscript questions; Production Coordinator for file or distribution issues.

Continuity Expectations: Every title must have a next action, owner, and follow-up date.

## 3. Editorial Lead

Role Purpose: Protects manuscript quality, author voice, and editorial readiness.

Daily Responsibilities: Review manuscripts, author responses, editorial questions, and readiness blockers.

Weekly Responsibilities: Report editorial stage, author review status, unresolved questions, and source-file risks.

Monthly Responsibilities: Review editorial standards, recurring author questions, and handoff quality.

Approvals Held: Editorial preparation within approved scope and recommendation drafting.

Approvals Not Held: Final editorial master approval, sensitive author release, title repositioning, agreement changes, or scope expansion.

Top SOPs: Identify the Controlling Manuscript; Begin Developmental Editing; Prepare an Author Review Package; Process Author Revisions; Approve the Editorial Master.

Key Reports: Editorial queue, author review packages, unresolved questions.

Escalation Path: Jackie for final approval or sensitive decisions; Publishing Coordinator for author communication logistics.

Continuity Expectations: Preserve source clarity and make editorial status understandable to non-editors.

## 4. Production Coordinator

Role Purpose: Turns approved source text into approved editions.

Daily Responsibilities: Track proof status, file readiness, metadata needs, distribution blockers, and live-status checks.

Weekly Responsibilities: Report production stage, files awaiting approval, channel issues, and release obligations.

Monthly Responsibilities: Review title health, production quality issues, and post-release correction needs.

Approvals Held: Routine production preparation within approved scope.

Approvals Not Held: Edition additions, title changes, release exceptions, corrections, public-offer changes, or final live announcements.

Top SOPs: Begin Production; Prepare Paperback, Hardcover, and Ebook Editions; Confirm Metadata and ISBN Readiness; Prepare a Title for Distribution; Confirm a Title Is Live.

Key Reports: Production blockers, distribution readiness, live-status proof.

Escalation Path: Jackie for release or scope decisions; Editorial Lead for source text; Author Relations for author-facing messages.

Continuity Expectations: Each edition must have clear source, proof, approval, and live-status evidence.

## 5. Author Relations / Communications

Role Purpose: Keeps authors informed clearly and truthfully.

Daily Responsibilities: Review author messages, send approved updates, track due responses, and flag sensitive communication.

Weekly Responsibilities: Report authors waiting on J Merrill Publishing and authors who owe responses.

Monthly Responsibilities: Review communication quality, stale follow-ups, and author-copy communication obligations.

Approvals Held: Standard status updates and routine follow-up within approved language.

Approvals Not Held: Special promises, agreement changes, release announcements, sensitive editorial messages, or disputed issues.

Top SOPs: Start Author Onboarding; Send an Author Review Package; Process Author Revisions; Manage Complimentary Author Copies; Process Author-Copy Orders.

Key Reports: Author waiting list, overdue communications, author-copy obligations.

Escalation Path: Jackie for sensitive or binding messages; Publishing Coordinator for current title status.

Continuity Expectations: Authors should never need internal language to understand where their book stands.

## 6. Financial and Royalty Support

Role Purpose: Protects payment, royalty, and author-copy financial obligations.

Daily Responsibilities: Watch urgent payment or royalty issues and author-copy orders requiring payment review.

Weekly Responsibilities: Report agreement or payment exceptions and upcoming obligations.

Monthly Responsibilities: Prepare royalty review, payment evidence, unresolved allocation questions, and author-copy obligation review.

Approvals Held: Preparation and reconciliation within approved financial policy.

Approvals Not Held: Royalty term changes, payment exceptions, discounts, free extra copies, disputed payments, or agreement changes.

Top SOPs: Issue a Publishing Agreement; Manage Complimentary Author Copies; Process Author-Copy Orders; Close or Retire a Title.

Key Reports: Monthly royalties, payment exceptions, author-copy obligations.

Escalation Path: Jackie for exceptions; counsel or external support where required.

Continuity Expectations: Preserve financial evidence and never rely on memory for obligations.

## 7. Technology Administrator

Role Purpose: Supports the tools that hold publishing records without becoming the publishing decision owner.

Daily Responsibilities: Respond to access, file availability, broken link, and storage issues.

Weekly Responsibilities: Confirm operators can reach current documents and active work views.

Monthly Responsibilities: Review access hygiene, backup concerns, and support requests from operators.

Approvals Held: Routine support for access and preservation.

Approvals Not Held: Publishing decisions, agreement changes, public offers, title movement, author communication, or automation thaw.

Top SOPs: Run Today; Current Work; Emergency and Continuity; Legacy Boundary.

Key Reports: Access issues, preservation concerns, support tickets.

Escalation Path: Jackie for business authority; appropriate support owner for platform incidents.

Continuity Expectations: Keep current authorities available and protect evidence without changing business meaning.
