# 07 Reports and Reviews

This is the human operating rhythm for Publishing.

## Daily Review

Check:

- author responses;
- overdue actions;
- production blockers;
- urgent Jackie approvals;
- wrong-file or wrong-attachment risks;
- distribution listing concerns;
- payment or royalty items due soon.

Daily result:

- every active title has a next action or a blocker;
- every author waiting on J Merrill Publishing has a follow-up plan;
- urgent issues are escalated.

## Weekly Review

Check:

- active portfolio;
- title-stage reconciliation;
- authors waiting on J Merrill Publishing;
- upcoming release obligations;
- agreement or payment exceptions;
- distribution exceptions;
- author-copy obligations;
- titles without current-state summaries.

Weekly result:

- Jackie receives a short review of active titles, blockers, and decisions needed.

## Monthly Review

Check:

- royalties;
- active title health;
- publication progress;
- catalog and offer accuracy;
- title retirement or reversion candidates;
- author-copy obligations;
- unresolved operational risk.

Monthly result:

- financial, release, and author-facing obligations are current or escalated.

## Quarterly Review

Check:

- portfolio health;
- policy accuracy;
- SOP usefulness;
- template and version accuracy;
- successor readiness;
- drift between the manual process and the actual work.

Quarterly result:

- update recommendations are prepared for Jackie, but no policy changes occur without approval.
