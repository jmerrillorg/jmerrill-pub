# 05 Decisions and Approvals

This guide answers who decides.

## Jackie Decisions

Jackie decides:

- Publishing Track;
- title acceptance;
- title decline;
- special terms;
- agreement exceptions;
- Format & Title Lock;
- Correction Authorized;
- release exceptions;
- public-offer changes;
- client automation thaw;
- royalty or payment exceptions;
- author-copy benefit exceptions;
- title closeout, retirement, or reversion.

## Operator Decisions

Operators may handle:

- routine follow-up;
- evidence filing;
- package preparation;
- scheduling;
- checklist completion;
- standard status updates;
- routine title movement under approved policy;
- identifying blockers;
- preparing decision summaries for Jackie.

Operators may not change rights, royalties, prices, public promises, agreements, package benefits, release exceptions, or automation boundaries.

## Author Decisions

Authors decide or confirm:

- title and subtitle;
- author-name presentation;
- editorial review responses;
- cover approval where applicable;
- Product Form elections where the approved track permits author choice;
- required revisions;
- release approvals where applicable;
- delivery information for author copies.

## External Decisions

External decision owners may include:

- distributor;
- vendor;
- rights holder;
- counsel;
- government or institutional partner;
- payment provider;
- fulfillment partner.

## Escalation Rule

When authority is unclear:

1. Pause the affected action.
2. Preserve the current state.
3. Identify the decision owner.
4. Do not invent a workaround.
5. Brief Jackie with the decision needed, the title affected, the risk, and the recommended next step.
