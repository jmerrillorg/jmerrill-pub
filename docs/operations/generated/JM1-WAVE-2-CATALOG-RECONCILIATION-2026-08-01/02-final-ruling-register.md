# Final Ruling Register

Authority: Priority Wave 2 governance execution.

## Ruling Vocabulary

- MIGRATE: carry forward as an active catalog record after implementation authorization.
- AMEND: carry forward only with the listed correction.
- MERGE: preserve the historical SKU and migrate operational authority to the surviving equivalent.
- PROVISIONAL: hold from activation until missing governance facts are resolved.
- RETIRE: no rows received this ruling.

## Required Operational Controls

- No provisional SKU may be made author-facing without a subsequent Jackie ruling.
- No superseded SKU may be recreated as a parallel active offer.
- No amended SKU may be activated in a way that duplicates an existing governed fee or package scope.
- Historical identifiers remain searchable for audit and support.

The row-level rulings are in 01-authoritative-reconciled-worksheet.csv and the implementation crosswalk is in 03-migration-crosswalk.csv.
