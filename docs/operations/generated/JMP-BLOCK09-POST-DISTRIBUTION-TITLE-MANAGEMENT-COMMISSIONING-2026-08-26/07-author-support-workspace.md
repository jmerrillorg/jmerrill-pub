# Published Author Support And Workspace

Last Verified: 2026-08-26

## Customer Service

Dynamics 365 Customer Service is the governed first path for published-author support cases.

Case relationship:

AUTHOR + TITLE + EDITION + ISSUE/REQUEST.

Royalty or payment questions may create governed cases but are routed to JACKIE_REVIEW_REQUIRED. The runtime does not send royalty amounts, payment timing, or payment scheduling responses.

## Email To Case

Author email to publishing@jmerrill.one can be preserved and associated to the correct author/title/edition case. The author is not asked to repeat information already supplied.

## Author Workspace Published Title Home

Visible sections include publication status, formats, verified buy links, sales summary, royalty statements, payment status, marketing resources, reviews/media, files, copy orders, corrections, updates, and future edition/format requests.

Internal accounting entries, raw distributor errors, internal payment rails, and sensitive bank/tax details are not exposed.
