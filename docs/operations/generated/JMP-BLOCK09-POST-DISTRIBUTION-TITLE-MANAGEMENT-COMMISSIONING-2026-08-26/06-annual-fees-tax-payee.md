# Annual Fees, Tax, And Payee Administration

Last Verified: 2026-08-26

## Annual Distribution Fee

Current default implemented:

$30 per print format annually, subject to executed agreement, policy version, waiver, exception, or title-specific term.

Paperback and hardcover may create separate obligations. eBook and audio do not inherit print-format fees without authority.

## Annual Fee Clock

States:

UPCOMING, DUE, PAID, OVERDUE, WAIVED, NO_LONGER_APPLICABLE.

No real author annual fee invoice was sent for commissioning.

## Tax / Payee

Author, payee, rights holder, successor, estate, and beneficiary are modeled separately where governed. Tax administration supports evidence and readiness without inventing thresholds, forms, withholding rules, or legal classifications inside Publishing code.
