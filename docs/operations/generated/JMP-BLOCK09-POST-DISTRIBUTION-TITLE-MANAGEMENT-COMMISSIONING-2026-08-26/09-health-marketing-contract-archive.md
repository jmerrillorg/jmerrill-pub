# Health, Marketing, Contract, And Archive

Last Verified: 2026-08-26

## Title Health

Domains:

- Distribution
- Financial
- Metadata
- Marketing
- Contract
- Author Service

Commercial performance is separate from title health. Low sales alone do not make a title unhealthy, and high sales do not hide operational defects.

## Evergreen Marketing

Block 09 consumes Block 08 handoff intelligence: strongest messages, assets, audience insights, campaign history, metrics, reviews/social proof, publicity placements, verified links, and open opportunities.

## Contract / Rights

Executed title contract controls. Low sales alone does not automatically trigger reversion. Reversion is a review path, not an automatic mutation.

## Terminal State Separation

Title retirement, rights reversion, and distribution takedown remain separate facts.

## Archive

Archive is denied while required financial, rights, file, or distribution obligations remain unresolved. Archive preserves history; it does not delete the title.
