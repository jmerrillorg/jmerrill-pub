# JM1 Holdings Purpose Statement

Status: COMPLETED WITH NAMED EXTERNAL DEPENDENCY

## Approved Business Purpose

JM1 Holdings is a contemplated future governance and ownership structure for coordinating enterprise-level ownership, stewardship, shared services, brand administration, intellectual-property strategy, and inter-entity operating standards across the J Merrill ecosystem.

It is intended to clarify enterprise purpose and future governance design. It is not an implemented legal entity, tax structure, ownership transfer, or operating company.

## Approved Non-Implementation Boundary

This package does not authorize:

- entity formation;
- ownership transfer;
- tax election;
- bank account creation;
- contract assignment;
- payroll or employment changes;
- asset transfer;
- legal representation that JM1 Holdings exists as a formed entity.

## Separate Boundary For AIC

Agape International Cathedral may be assessed for platform reuse and operational alignment, but remains legally and operationally separate. This purpose statement does not consolidate AIC into JM1 corporate ownership or control.
