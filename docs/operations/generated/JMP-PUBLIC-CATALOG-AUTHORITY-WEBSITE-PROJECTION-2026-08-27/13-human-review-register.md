# Human Review Register

Last Verified: 2026-08-27T22:56:00-04:00

Human review is still required when:

- public title identity conflicts;
- author identity or pen-name authority conflicts;
- legacy records cannot be deterministically linked;
- a title is legally/operationally terminal but still marked public/active;
- duplicate public slugs refer to different real works or people.

No human decision was fabricated by this work. The projection layer surfaces holds instead of resolving ambiguous identity.
