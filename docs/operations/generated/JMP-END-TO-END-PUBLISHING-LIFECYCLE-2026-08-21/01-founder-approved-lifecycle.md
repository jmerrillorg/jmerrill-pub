# 01 - Founder-Approved Lifecycle

Authority target: `JMP_PUBLISHING_LIFECYCLE_v1.0`

## Canonical 10-Stage Lifecycle

| Stage | Name | Lifecycle Dimension |
|---:|---|---|
| 01 | Inquiry & Intake | Prospect / Commercial |
| 02 | Classification | Prospect / Commercial |
| 03 | Editorial Review & Publishing Recommendation | Prospect / Commercial |
| 04 | Package Acceptance & Commercial Activation | Prospect / Commercial |
| 05 | Join the Family & Author Onboarding | Author Relationship |
| 06A | Editorial Production - Developmental Editing | Title |
| 06B | Editorial Production - Line Editing | Title |
| 06C | Editorial Production - Copyediting | Title |
| 07 | Book Production - Layout, Proofreading, Final Author Approval, Production Finalization | Title |
| 08 | Cover, Metadata & Distribution Readiness | Title / Edition |
| 09 | Distribution, Launch & Release | Title / Edition |
| 10 | Post-Publication Title & Author Relationship | Author Relationship / Title Stewardship |

## Required Stage Anatomy

Every canonical stage must define entry condition, source of truth, work, governing artifact/data, quality gate, waiting owner, system attention behavior, and exit contract.

## Naming Clarification

Current repo evidence uses J0-J8, Editorial Review, Developmental, Line, Copyediting, Proofreading, Interior Layout, Cover Design, Production Proof, Production Handoff, Backlist/Published, and Ongoing Relationship. These names should be adapted into the ten-stage canon. Founder intent does not require changing the ten stages; it requires binding current names to canonical stage/substage codes.

## Parallelism Rule

Parallelize preparation. Serialize irreversible gates. Cover preparation, metadata drafting, payment setup, and workspace readiness may begin before all prior gates complete only when the registry marks the work as preparatory and non-irreversible.
