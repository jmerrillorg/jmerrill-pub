# Human-First Author Gates

The synchronized manual canon requires author review/approval between editorial
stages:

- Developmental -> Author Review/Approval -> Line
- Line -> Author Review/Approval -> Copy
- Copy -> Author Review/Approval -> Layout / Typesetting
- Proof -> Final Author Approval -> Production Finalization

Approval is channel-agnostic. A valid author decision may be captured through
email, Author Operating Center, phone, in person, Teams video, SMS, or another
recorded channel.

Offline/verbal decisions must distinguish:

- `Decision Made By = Author`
- `Recorded By = Publisher operator`

Every approval binds to the exact artifact/version/checksum under review.
