# Cover And Distribution Alignment

## Cover

The synchronized Cover Intelligence cache distinguishes:

- Cover Concept
- Full Wrap
- Final Production Cover

`COVER_CONCEPT_APPROVED` does not equal `FULL_WRAP_APPROVED` or
`FINAL_COVER_APPROVED`.

Cover Intelligence must consider genre, target audience, product formats,
distribution path, trim, page count, binding, paper, spine/template authority,
barcode/ISBN requirements, and front/spine/back composition.

## Distribution

Distribution Review remains a distinct final readiness gate rather than an
editing stage. It evaluates metadata, categories, rights, ISBN, files,
accessibility, pricing, wholesale discount, returnability, print cost,
profitability/margin, distribution-path readiness, and sequencing.

A print format that fails required profitability/readiness cannot be marked
Ready for Distribution merely because files technically exist.
