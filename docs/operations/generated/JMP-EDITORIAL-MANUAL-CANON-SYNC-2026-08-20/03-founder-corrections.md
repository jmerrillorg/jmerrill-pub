# Founder Corrections Applied

## Imprint Nonblocking

A missing confirmed imprint does not block Editorial Review. If a confirmed
imprint exists, use it. Otherwise determine and use the suggested imprint and
continue.

## JM Signature Exception

JM Signature suggested does not equal JM Signature officially assigned.
Editorial Review proceeds, but official assignment requires Publisher approval.
The cache now names the equivalent state:
`JM_SIGNATURE_RECOMMENDED_PENDING_PUBLISHER_APPROVAL`.

## Author Approval

Author review/approval is required between editorial stages. No AI completion
event substitutes for author approval.

## Channel-Agnostic Approval

Author approval is mandatory; electronic self-service approval is not. A valid
author decision may be captured through governed channels including email,
Author Operating Center, phone, in person, Teams video, SMS, or another
recorded channel.

When Publishing records an offline/verbal decision, the canon distinguishes
`Decision Made By = Author` from `Recorded By = Publisher operator`.

## Artifact Binding

Every approval binds to the exact artifact/version/checksum under review. If an
artifact changes materially after approval, that approval does not
automatically transfer.

## Proof Post-Layout

Proofreading is post-copyedit and post-layout/typesetting. Proof checks
layout-introduced errors and requires Final Author Approval before Production
Finalization.

## Cover State Distinction

Cover Concept, Full Wrap, and Final Production Cover are distinct. Concept
approval does not equal full-wrap approval or final-cover approval.
