# Author Decision Semantics

Last verified: 2026-08-15T10:35:00-04:00

## Separate Decisions

Editorial approval and title selection are separate decisions.

The author may:

- approve Editorial Review;
- provide a preferred title;
- select one suggested title;
- decline/defer title selection and keep `Untitled` for now.

Keeping `Untitled` does not block Editorial Review approval or ordinary next editorial-stage authorization.

## Canonical Title Mutation

Title truth mutates only when the author provides or selects a title. The decision records:

- prior title;
- new title;
- source = author-provided or author-selected suggestion;
- author/gate/source context;
- title status.

Suggested titles do not become canonical until explicit author selection.
