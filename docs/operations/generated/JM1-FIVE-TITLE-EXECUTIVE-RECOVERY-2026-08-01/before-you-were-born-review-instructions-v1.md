# Before You Were Born Author Review Instructions

Package version: `BYWB-DEVELOPMENTAL-AUTHOR-REVIEW-2026-08-01-v1`

Stage: Developmental Editing

## What To Review

Please review the Developmental Editing package for Before You Were Born. This review is focused on developmental direction, not final grammar, page layout, or production formatting.

Please inspect:

- the edited manuscript or current governed manuscript reference;
- the Developmental memo;
- the author-facing Developmental summary;
- any recommendations, questions, or decisions identified by Publishing;
- the package manifest.

## Appropriate Responses At This Stage

You may:

- approve the package as presented;
- approve the package with a consolidated correction list;
- request clarification about one or more recommendations.

Please send one consolidated response. This keeps the title record clean and prevents duplicate or conflicting approval gates.

## Changes Requiring Additional Publishing Review

Substantial rewriting, new chapters, major structural changes, new claims, legal-sensitive content, or material scope changes may require separate Publishing review before the title advances.

## Response Period

The response period is seven calendar days from confirmed delivery of the package.

The response clock starts only after Publishing confirms successful delivery and archive evidence.

## What Happens Next

After Publishing receives your response, the team will reconcile your decision, preserve the approval or correction evidence, and move the project through the next governed stage-transition path.

For help, contact Publishing at publishing@jmerrill.one.
