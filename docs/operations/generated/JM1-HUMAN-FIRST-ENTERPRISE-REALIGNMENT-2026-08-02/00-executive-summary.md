# JM1 Human-First Enterprise Realignment

**Date:** 2026-08-02
**Authority:** Jackie Smith, Jr.
**Status:** Prepared, validated, and approved for canonicalization

## Executive Result

The enterprise vision is reaffirmed:

- People first.
- Legacy always.
- Technology in service to both.

This package prepares the `JM1 HUMAN-FIRST SERVICE DELIVERY STANDARD` for canonical repository authority and freezes nonessential new platform work until the highest-risk human-experience failures are closed.

## Governing Finding

JM1 has repeatedly proven technical capability, but several active workflows had begun treating system success as the same thing as human success. That distinction is now formally corrected.

The new completion test is:

> No technical transaction is complete unless the person being served can understand, access, use, and respond to the delivered outcome.

## Immediate Operating Change

For ordinary client and author service:

- email, phone, and direct document delivery are primary;
- portals are optional enhancements unless specifically justified;
- internal package artifacts stay internal;
- plain-language communication is required;
- human fallback is required;
- operational success is measured by the person's ability to use the outcome.

## Highest-Risk Recovery Items

1. Five live Publishing titles waiting on current-stage author packages.
2. Author-package communication and delivery maturity.
3. Author Operating Center becoming a prerequisite instead of a support surface.
4. Financial scheduling and client calendar authority remaining clear to clients.
5. Public website and intake pages retaining Why-First language and simple support paths.

## New Work Freeze

Held until critical human-experience failures close:

- new infrastructure modernization;
- new pipeline programs;
- new AI agents;
- new portals;
- new automation waves;
- Wave 3;
- nonessential architecture enhancements;
- additional author-package complexity.

Permitted:

- urgent client and author service;
- production incident correction;
- safe completion of current obligations;
- usability remediation;
- human-first communication improvements;
- evidence required to support this review.
