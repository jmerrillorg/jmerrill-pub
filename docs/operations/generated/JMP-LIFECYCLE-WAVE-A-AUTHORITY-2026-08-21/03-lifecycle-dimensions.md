# 03 - Lifecycle Dimensions

The registry distinguishes three connected dimensions:

| Dimension | Registry value | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Prospect / Commercial | PROSPECT_COMMERCIAL | Inquiry, intake, classification, recommendation, package/commercial activation |
| Title | TITLE | Editorial production, book production, distribution, release, post-publication title stewardship |
| Author Relationship | AUTHOR_RELATIONSHIP | Joined the Family, active/returning/multi-title author relationship, loyalty/referral relationship |

The validator accepts valid combinations where commercial state, title state, and author relationship state differ. This prevents false contradictions such as a Returning Author having one title in Post-Publication and another title in Copyediting.
