# Attribution, Measurement, And Performance Review

Last Verified: 2026-08-26

## Attribution

Attribution confidence uses:

- `ATTRIBUTABLE`
- `CORRELATED`
- `UNKNOWN`

The runtime denies fabricated sales attribution when retailer/distributor evidence cannot prove purchase attribution.

## Measurement

Metrics include:

- reach;
- engagement;
- intent;
- commercial signal;
- earned impact.

Metrics must remain attached to campaign identity.

## Launch Performance Review

Block 08 cannot close because launch day passed or a sales target was met/missed.

The review captures what worked, what did not, strongest message, strongest channel, best assets, best CTA, audience response, earned media, reviews, author participation, paid media where applicable, commercial signals, weak channels, and ongoing opportunities.
