# Package, Scope, And Author Approval

Last Verified: 2026-08-26

## Package Entitlement

The runtime preserves package entitlement separately from title-specific Marketing Scope Lock.

Package mapping supports:

- `JMP-PKG-STARTER`
- `JMP-PKG-PRO`
- `JMP-PKG-PREMIER`
- `JM-SIGNATURE`

Paid media, ARC, and publicity lanes remain `ADD_ON_ONLY`, `SCOPE_DEPENDENT`, or `APPROVAL_REQUIRED` unless governed entitlement exists.

## Scope Lock

Marketing Scope Lock governs included JMP marketing, author participation, website, email, social, toolkit, preorder, ARC/reviews, publicity, events, paid media, campaign duration, assets, analytics, approval needs, owner, budget, add-ons, and scope version.

Silent scope expansion and silent scope reduction fail closed.

## Representation Risk

Routine low-risk assets may proceed under JMP approval. Medium/high representation-risk content requires author approval bound to exact campaign, asset, asset version, message version, and content state.

Changed high-risk content cannot inherit prior approval.
