# Block 09 Handoff And Surfaces

Last Verified: 2026-08-26

## Evergreen Handoff

Block 09 receives campaign history, current verified buy links, metrics, strongest messages, best assets, audience insights, review/social-proof registry, publicity placements, author participation history, campaign learnings, open opportunities, release health, and ongoing recommendations.

## Publisher Operating Center

The Publisher Operating Center exposes title, campaign phase, launch date, campaign health, release health, waiting-on, age, and alert state.

## Author Workspace

The Author Workspace exposes simplified campaign phase, launch date, participation requests, toolkit, and verified buy links.

It does not expose internal media pipeline, ad-platform errors, risk scoring, or technical analytics.
