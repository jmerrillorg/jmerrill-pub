# Assets, Calendar, And Activity

Last Verified: 2026-08-26

## Marketing Asset Registry

Block 08 registers governed marketing assets with:

- marketing asset ID;
- campaign ID;
- title ID;
- asset type;
- version;
- checksum;
- source;
- status;
- approval status;
- channel suitability;
- creation timestamp;
- supersession link.

Uncontrolled export folders are not authority.

## Asset Lineage

The synthetic lineage proves:

## Campaign Plan And Calendar

The campaign plan/calendar derive from publication date and Block 07 live state. Publication-date changes require calendar revalidation.

## Activity Contract

Significant campaign activities carry campaign ID, channel, owner, status, planned/due/executed dates, asset, message version, CTA, UTM parameters, waiting-on, and result.
