## Title Marketing Brief

The brief consumes governed upstream facts from Blocks 02-07, including title, author, imprint, audience, author goals, book goals, themes, positioning, comparable titles, author platform, formats, publication date, verified links, rights/territory, marketing entitlement, author participation, sensitivities, and constraints.

The author is not asked to re-enter governed data.

## Message House

The message house governs web, email, social, press, author toolkit, events, and paid media. Channel-specific invented truth is denied.
