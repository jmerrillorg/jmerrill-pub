# Channel, Consent, CTA, And Release Health

Last Verified: 2026-08-26

## Social

Dataverse/JMP remains campaign system of record. Meta or another external platform may be execution system only where actually required.

Wrong social account blocks execution. A social-platform restriction creates lane attention and does not stop healthy lanes.

## Email

Promotional email requires marketing consent.

Service communication consent is separate and does not require marketing consent.

## CTA Authority

Campaign CTAs consume Block 07 verified URL authority. Manually pasted unverified URLs are denied.

## Release Health

Release health and campaign health remain separate.

If one format has an incident, the affected CTA is suppressed while healthy alternatives can continue.
