# Founder-Approved Intake Canon

Hard invariants:

- A website success response requires durable intake persistence.
- Notification is downstream and supplemental.
- Dataverse remains the system of record.
- Manuscript is not required to create a valid inquiry.
- Manuscript is required before Editorial Review begins.
- Apple Pages is a supported source format.
- Original author files are preserved and checksummed.
- Returning authors are resolved against governed records downstream.
- Referral is attributed at intake but not earned until `JOINED_THE_FAMILY`.
- Full Author Workspace activates only after agreement execution and required initial payment.
