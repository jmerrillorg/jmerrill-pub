# Join Form Field Map

Implemented fields:

- Legal first and last name
- Preferred name, publishing/display name, pen name
- Email, phone, preferred communication, timezone
- Returning JMP author signal
- Mailing address and billing-address distinction
- Working title, subtitle, work type, genre, word count
- Manuscript status and manuscript now/later choice
- Manuscript file or shareable link
- Intended audience, goals, desired timeline, prior history
- Why JMP and publishing partner hope
- Referral attribution
- Discovery/marketing source
- Author platform
- Accessibility notes
- Rights attestation, third-party material disclosure, AI disclosure, sensitive content disclosure
- Required service communication consent
- Optional marketing consent
- UTM/referrer attribution captured in background

No public imprint selection is exposed. JM Signature is not exposed.
