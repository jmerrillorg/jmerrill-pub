# Subject And Body Semantics

Last Verified: 2026-08-21T08:24:00Z

## Canonical Subject Rule

Subjects must identify the book/project and the understandable reason for the message. They must not lead with an intake reference, GUID, diagnostic ID, artifact ID, gate ID, or execution identifier.

## Controlled Proof

| Message reference | Subject |
|---|---|
| JMP-INT-202608-CANON01 | We Received Your Publishing Inquiry for New Book Test |
| JMP-INT-202608-CANON02 | We Received Your Publishing Inquiry for New Book Test |

The intake reference appeared in the body for records, not in the subject.
