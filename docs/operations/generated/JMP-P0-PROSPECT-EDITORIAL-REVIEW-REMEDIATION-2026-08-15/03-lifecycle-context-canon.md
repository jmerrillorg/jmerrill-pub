# Lifecycle Context Canon

Last verified: 2026-08-15

Evidence source: `lib/server/publishing-lifecycle-context.ts`

Canonical contexts:

- `PROSPECT_INQUIRY`
- `ACTIVE_CONTRACTED_AUTHOR`

Rule:

Contact, title, opportunity, Author Workspace, diagnostic, artifact, or gate existence does not create active contracted-author status.

Promotion to active contracted-author requires governed commercial execution signals such as executed agreement plus payment, onboarding, or publishing authorization.
