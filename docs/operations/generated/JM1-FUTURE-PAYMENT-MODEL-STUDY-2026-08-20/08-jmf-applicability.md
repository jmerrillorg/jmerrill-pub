# J Merrill Financial Applicability

Date: 2026-08-20
Classification: STUDY / NO JMF ACTIVATION OR MUTATION

## Boundary

Publishing payment rules do not automatically transfer to J Merrill Financial. JMF must evaluate its own authority, regulatory posture, service model, and third-party arrangements before adopting any similar payment structure.

## Potentially Applicable

The future model may be studied for JMF-owned advisory or service fees where JMF itself controls:

- the service price;
- the payment schedule;
- the agreement language;
- delivery/release conditions;
- refund or payoff treatment;
- accounting treatment.

Examples may include internal JMF service packages if those services are governed as JMF-owned fees and not as carrier-controlled products.

## Not Automatically Applicable

The Publishing model must not be applied automatically to:

- insurance premiums;
- pre-need carrier payments;
- FDLIC arrangements;
- Precoa arrangements;
- carrier-controlled payment schedules;
- regulated third-party products;
- products where payment terms are set by another regulated entity.

## Review Required

Before JMF adopts anything similar, JMF needs a separate canon/authority review covering:

- who owns the fee;
- whether payment terms are advisory/service fees or regulated product payments;
- whether a third-party carrier controls schedule, billing, disclosure, cancellation, or payoff;
- whether JMF may collect, finance, or schedule payment for the item;
- what disclosures are required;
- what accounting and tax treatment applies.

## Recommendation

JMF should conduct a separate Financial-specific study for JMF-owned service fees only. It should not inherit the Publishing payment model by analogy.
