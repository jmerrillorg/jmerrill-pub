# Contract and Disclosure Impact

Date: 2026-08-20
Classification: STUDY / NO CONTRACT CHANGE

## Boundary

This study does not amend any existing agreement, addendum, payment schedule, disclosure, author-facing document, or package price. Existing executed agreements remain governed by their executed terms.

## Disclosure Topics

Future author-facing language would need to answer:

- What is the cash price?
- What is the selected payment plan?
- What is the plan charge, finance charge, or rate?
- What is the total cost if the plan runs to completion?
- What happens if the author pays early?
- Which charges are earned and which future charges are not collected after payoff?
- What must be paid before final release or deliverable handoff?

## Existing Contracts

Existing contracts should not be regenerated, rewritten, or silently conformed to a future model. Future changes to executed agreements require amendment, addendum, or superseding agreement through approved contract governance.

## Legal/Compliance Boundary

This file identifies review needs only. It does not provide a legal conclusion on consumer-credit law, lending, finance charges, usury, disclosure sufficiency, tax treatment, or accounting treatment.
