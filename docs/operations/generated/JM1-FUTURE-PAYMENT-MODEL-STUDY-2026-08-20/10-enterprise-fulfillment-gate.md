# Enterprise Fulfillment Gate Study

Date: 2026-08-20
Classification: STUDY / POLICY CANDIDATE

## Candidate Policy

Work may progress while an approved payment plan is active, but final product or deliverable release remains subject to completion of the contractual payment obligation.

## Publishing Applicability

Publishing is the strongest fit for this rule.

Recommended Publishing interpretation:

- onboarding and production work may continue during an active approved plan;
- routine internal work should not stop solely because the plan has not completed;
- payment exceptions should surface promptly;
- final delivery/release is blocked until the contractual payment obligation is satisfied or an authorized exception exists.

Examples of final release/deliverable gates may include:

- final production file release;
- public publication/release authorization;
- final author deliverable package;
- post-production deliverable release where payment completion is required.

## Financial Applicability

Potentially applicable only to JMF-owned advisory/service fees. Not automatically applicable to third-party regulated products, insurance premiums, carrier-controlled schedules, FDLIC arrangements, or Precoa arrangements.

## Foundation Paid Programs

If the Foundation operates paid programs, the rule may apply to final deliverables or access completion. Exceptions may be needed for charitable, grant-funded, donor-funded, or mission-access programs where payment is not the controlling release condition.

## Productions

Likely applicable to JM1-owned production services, media deliverables, edits, final cuts, source files, licenses, and release packages. Exceptions may be needed where contract terms define milestone delivery differently.

## Other JM1 Fee-for-Service Offerings

Likely applicable where JM1 controls the fee, work, and deliverable. Must be checked by division and agreement type.

## Exceptions to Define

- executive waiver;
- hardship or mission exception;
- contract-specific release right;
- regulated third-party product;
- carrier-controlled payment;
- author/client refund or cancellation state;
- legal hold;
- dispute state;
- internal validation or pilot title with no client deliverable.

## Recommendation

Adopt the rule as a future enterprise policy candidate only after each division defines its exceptions and source-of-authority boundaries.
