# Single-Operator Proof

Last verified: 2026-08-10T02:46:15Z

Question: If another author approves a proof tomorrow, does Jackie need to manually reconcile the decision, awaiting state, artifact checksum, or closeout evidence?

Answer: NO for normal matched/approved cases.

Jackie remains required only when:

- the reply is ambiguous;
- the artifact is ambiguous;
- correlation fails;
- the decision is exceptional;
- Tier 4 or business judgment is required;
- the protected title-state mutation itself is ready for executive authorization.
