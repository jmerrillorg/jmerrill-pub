# JM Signature Attorney Review Packet

## Business Decisions For Counsel Review

1. JM Signature is invitation-only and not a public package.
2. JM Signature remains blocked from author use until attorney review and Jackie ratification.
3. The current business terms are considered ready for counsel review, not final legal enforcement.
4. Counsel should review royalty, fee, and funding language for enforceability and consumer/business compliance.
5. Counsel should review term, renewal, termination, cure, and survival language.
6. Counsel should review any exclusivity or non-compete language before author use.
7. Counsel should review rights, reversion, derivative-rights, and territory language.
8. Counsel should review AI narration, AI assistance, voice, likeness, and manuscript-processing clauses.
9. Counsel should review governing law, venue, notices, and dispute-resolution language.
10. Counsel should review author warranties, disclaimers, indemnity, limitation of liability, and responsibility allocation.
11. Counsel should identify any provisions that require separate initials, disclosures, or plain-language presentation.
12. Counsel should return redlines and a final approval recommendation before operational activation.

## Six Counsel Questions

1. Are the current author obligations, payment, and service-delivery provisions enforceable as drafted?
2. Does the agreement properly distinguish publishing services, funding, royalties, reimbursements, and optional add-ons?
3. Are the rights, reversion, derivative-rights, and media-rights clauses sufficient for JM Signature's intended use?
4. Are AI narration and AI-assisted editorial provisions legally sufficient and appropriately disclosed?
5. Are term, renewal, termination, cure, and non-compete or exclusivity provisions acceptable under applicable law?
6. What changes are required before the agreement can be sent to an author?
